About the Pittsburgh Steelers

The Pittsburgh Steelers are a professional American football team based in Pittsburg, Pennsylvania. They compete in the National Football League (NFL) and are members of the American Football Conference (AFC) North division.

History

  • Founded in 1933
  • Won 6 Super Bowl titles
  • Known for their strong defense

Features

The Pittsburg Steelers are known for their really strong defense and disciplined play. They have a loyal fan base and a really good history in the NFL.
